- **1d.** Tagged all 7 VoC pilot records as missing-capability vs. bug-in-existing-feature.

## Atomic dictionary gaps — triaged, not individually carded

The remaining dictionary-tracked IDs not absorbed into a headline candidate above are single-checkbox-sized items (one UI control, one option, one format) rather than a cohesive "feature to widen the lead." Carrying each of the unverified + remaining confirmed-absent/present-elsewhere IDs as a full 5-axis card would misrepresent their scale relative to the 20 headline candidates. They are listed here in full for completeness (the "don't silently drop coverage" principle), grouped by feature area, and are **not** expected to survive to the shortlist on Reach/Severity grounds alone — Stage 2 gives them a single blanket assessment rather than dozens of individual ones.
